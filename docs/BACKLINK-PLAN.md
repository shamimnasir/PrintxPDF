# PrintxPDF backlink and external discovery plan

Updated 2026-10-04. This is a quality-first outreach log, not a target count. A directory submission is not an earned backlink until it is approved and the public listing links to PrintxPDF.

## Guardrails

- Prefer relevant editorial, product, education, and community references that can send real visitors.
- Submit PrintxPDF once per product directory, linking to `/tools`. Do not submit all 44 tools as duplicate products. Tool-specific links should come from pages that genuinely explain or recommend that specific workflow.
- Do not buy ranking links, automate directory submissions, exchange links at scale, or add optimized links to comments and profiles. Google lists low-quality directories, automated link creation, and excessive exchanges as link spam. Paid placements need `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`.
- A pending form, a search result, or a nofollow listing is not a confirmed followed backlink.
- Create accounts or accept terms only when the owner explicitly authorizes directory submissions. Do not solve or bypass human challenges. Do not buy a listing or promotion without explicit payment approval.

## Where individual tool-page links can come from

Generic directories generally accept one listing per product and should point to the complete tools index. To earn links to individual pages, prioritize original, useful contributions where the tool is the destination that directly answers the question: classroom or library guides, accessibility and document-workflow resources, WordPress tutorials, extension coverage, and independent comparisons that actually test the workflow. Offer a specific tool page only when it is the best fit for the surrounding content. Do not ask publishers to use keyword-stuffed anchor text or require a followed link.

Useful assets to offer publishers include a short tested workflow, before-and-after screenshots without personal data, clear file-processing disclosures, and an embeddable link to the exact relevant tool. Outreach should be individualized and transparent, not a mass email blast.

## Measurement

1. Recheck the two pending directory submissions after a reasonable review period and record their public listing URLs and link attributes.
2. In Search Console's Links report, record linking domains and top linked pages after Google refreshes its data. Do not treat Search Console as real-time; its link sample is not exhaustive.
3. Measure referral sessions and activated tool use in analytics, not just link counts.
4. Track `/tools/<slug>` separately so earned deep links can be distinguished from homepage or tools-index links.

Google references: [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies), [qualifying outbound links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links).
